🎄 Villainous White Elephant 🎄

The Spirit of Christmas has wrapped a villain in every box. No peeking! Unwrap a present — or steal one that's already open.

How to play
  1. Everyone rolls a d20 — highest goes first.
  2. On your turn, unwrap a present or steal a villain someone already has.
  3. If your villain is stolen, unwrap a new present or steal from someone else — but not straight back.
  4. A villain can be stolen at most twice; after the second steal it's 🔒 locked.
  5. Lump of Coal (optional): whoever opens it must immediately steal from someone. No take-backs.
